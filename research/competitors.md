# Competitor watch — private club photography

Maintained monthly by task 2.4. Seeded 2026-08-05.

## The category exists, and that is good news

Member portrait days and club directory photography are an **established, named service
category**, not something we'd be inventing. Clubs already understand the ask. That removes
the hardest part of the sale — explaining what the thing is — and moves the conversation
straight to who does it and how well.

It also means we are never the only option, so the proposal has to be specific about what
we do differently.

## Known national/regional players

| Company | Positioning | What we know |
|---|---|---|
| **Lumatic Imagery** (lumaticimagery.com/for-country-clubs) | Country-club-specific photography, tech-forward | Member photos auto-tagged, searchable, shared; every member gets a headshot for their profile and the club directory. Sells the *system*, not the portrait. |
| **Trident Image Group** (tridentimagegroup.com/privateclub) | Full-service private club imagery | Course photography, racquet/pool, food photography, consistent headshots for staff and board. Sells *breadth* — one vendor for everything visual. |
| **Headshot Crew** | Photographer network | Not a club vendor per se, but a network clubs may source from. |

## What their positioning tells us

Both incumbents lead with **infrastructure**: databases, tagging, searchability, coverage of
every visual need a club has. That's a real advantage against a solo studio on volume — and
a real weakness on the thing members actually care about.

Neither of them sells the portrait. They sell the pipeline the portrait travels through.

Our wedge, stated plainly:

- **Their product is a headshot. Ours is a portrait.** A directory tile that looks like a
  badge photo is what members quietly hate and avoid. The whole Meta deck is built on the
  observation that people believe they photograph badly — `posing-is-not-your-job` is the
  answer to that, and no infrastructure vendor is even in that conversation.
- **A named photographer beats a dispatched one.** Julian directs the sitter. A vendor
  network sends whoever is available. Clubs whose entire value proposition is *we know you
  by name* respond to that argument, because it's their own argument.
- **The family sits too.** `every-generation` — spouse, children, grandchildren in the
  frame — turns a directory obligation into something a member actually wants. That's an
  order-value difference, and it's not a service the headshot vendors offer.
- **We are local and we will show up in person.** Neither incumbent is going to drive to
  Shingle Springs to shoot the management team for free.

## Where they beat us and we should not pretend otherwise

Multi-site portfolios, national management-company contracts, and any club that wants a
single vendor for course, food, event, and member imagery. If a club's brief is "cover
everything visual, all year," we're not the fit — and a proposal that pretends otherwise
loses on delivery instead of losing at the pitch, which is worse.
