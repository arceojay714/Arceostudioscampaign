# Working assumptions

Every automated task encodes guesses about this business. Left unchecked they drift away
from reality quietly, and the tasks keep running as if nothing changed. Reviewed monthly —
correct anything wrong and the tasks that depend on it get updated the same day.

Seeded 2026-08-05.

## About the offer

- Members sit at no charge; revenue comes from optional print orders beyond the
  complimentary 5x7. **Assumed from the Tier 3 ad creative — confirm this is the standing
  offer and not a promotion.**
- Sittings run about 15 minutes. *Unverified.*
- Retouching is included in every package. *Stated in `posing-is-not-your-job` creative.*
- Proofs go out within a week of the shoot. *Unverified — this appears in draft proposals
  and becomes a promise the moment one is sent.*
- Julian shoots everything personally. This is load-bearing for the whole pitch. If it ever
  stops being true, the competitive argument in `research/competitors.md` collapses.

## About capacity

- One photographer. A portrait week is roughly [N] sittings — **unknown, and it determines
  whether Tier B travel is viable at all.**
- Turnaround capacity during a busy fall is unknown. Booking three clubs in October may not
  be physically possible.
- Free management shoots cost a morning plus prints. Sustainable at maybe two a month.

## About the market

- Four home markets: Olympia, Kent, Cameron Park, Piedmont. Tier A is anything drivable
  from these.
- Roughly 5,659 private clubs nationally. Tier C is a signal net, not a service commitment.
- Fall is directory season, so outreach must land in late spring. *Reasoned, not verified —
  worth asking one friendly GM directly.*

## About the tools

- Email sends from a single mailbox. Volume above ~10 cold contacts a week risks
  deliverability.
- No CRM. `outreach/prospects.csv` and `ops/sessions.csv` are the system of record. If a
  real CRM appears, the tasks need rewriting to read from it.
- No task sends anything. If that ever changes, every guardrail in the playbook needs
  revisiting first.
