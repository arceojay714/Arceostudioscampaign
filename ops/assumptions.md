# Working assumptions

Every automated task encodes guesses about this business. Left unchecked they drift away
from reality quietly, and the tasks keep running as if nothing changed. Reviewed monthly —
correct anything wrong and the tasks that depend on it get updated the same day.

Seeded 2026-08-05.

## About the offer

- Members sit at no charge; revenue comes from optional print orders beyond the
  complimentary 5x7. **Assumed from the Tier 3 ad creative — confirm this is the standing
  offer and not a promotion.**
- Sittings run about 15 minutes. *Unverified.*
- Retouching is included in every package. *Stated in `posing-is-not-your-job` creative.*
- Proofs go out within a week of the shoot. *Unverified — this appears in draft proposals
  and becomes a promise the moment one is sent.*
- Julian shoots everything personally. This is load-bearing for the whole pitch. If it ever
  stops being true, the competitive argument in `research/competitors.md` collapses.

## About the network

- Ten photographers plus Jay, across nine metros. **None are onboarded, contracted, or
  insured yet** — the roster is a list of willing people, not available capacity.
- Commission-only was the stated intent. `docs/photographer-network.md` argues for a
  per-session floor plus commission instead; **unresolved, and it determines retention.**
- Assumed each photographer wants shoot days without a sales burden. Worth confirming
  individually — some may want the sales role and the higher earnings that come with it.
- **Worker classification is unresolved and is the largest structural risk in the plan.**
  Six of ten are in California under AB5; Connecticut applies its own ABC test. Needs an
  employment lawyer before the first commission check, not before the first outreach.
- Assumed central retouching and central sales. Both are unbuilt.
- No capture standard exists yet. Until one does, "consistent output across ten
  photographers" is an aspiration, not a capability.

## About capacity

- One photographer *today*. A portrait week is roughly [N] sittings — **unknown, and it determines
  whether Tier B travel is viable at all.**
- Turnaround capacity during a busy fall is unknown. Booking three clubs in October may not
  be physically possible.
- Free management shoots cost a morning plus prints. Sustainable at maybe two a month.

## About the market

- Four home markets: Olympia, Kent, Cameron Park, Piedmont. Tier A is anything drivable
  from these.
- Roughly 5,659 private clubs nationally. Tier C is a signal net, not a service commitment.
- Fall is directory season, so outreach must land in late spring. *Reasoned, not verified —
  worth asking one friendly GM directly.*

## About the tools

- Email sends from a single mailbox. Volume above ~10 cold contacts a week risks
  deliverability.
- No CRM. `outreach/prospects.csv` and `ops/sessions.csv` are the system of record. If a
  real CRM appears, the tasks need rewriting to read from it.
- No task sends anything. If that ever changes, every guardrail in the playbook needs
  revisiting first.

## Open questions for Jay

1. What is the actual member-facing price list beyond the complimentary print?
2. How many sittings can you do in a day, and how many days in a row?
3. Is there existing club work to reference, or does the reference list start at zero?
   (The roster may already have relevant work — worth asking all ten.)
4. Is the insurance in place, and can the broker name a club as additional insured?
5. Is `@thearceostudio` ever going to be connected to the business, or do we plan around
   the Facebook Page identity permanently?
6. Do photographers work under the Arceo Studio Collective name, or their own? This decides
   whether reviews, portfolios and referrals compound into one brand or ten.
7. Who pays for lab costs, travel, and the complimentary print — the studio or the
   photographer's commission? Unanswered, this becomes an argument after the first invoice.
