# Member Portraits for Private Clubs

A current, consistent portrait of every member, in the systems your staff already use, so
they can greet members by name.

Arceo Studio Collective runs on-site member portrait programs for private clubs. Julian
Arceo sets up in a private room at your clubhouse and photographs members against a
consistent hand-painted backdrop. Your club receives a directory portrait of each member
for its membership roster, point-of-sale system, and staff training directory. Members pay
no sitting fee.

**[Talk to Julian about your club →](mailto:julian@arceostudiocollective.com?subject=Member%20portraits%20at%20our%20club)**

<!-- H2 -->
## Every name is on file. Most faces aren't.

Your roster already holds each member's name, account, and locker. What it's often missing
is a current photo. Without one, a server in their first month can't greet a twenty-year
member by name, and members who have shared a clubhouse for years still have to introduce
themselves.

A portrait program fills that gap in a single round of sittings. It also gives members a
reason to show up, which a request to "send us a photo" doesn't.

## What your members receive

- **No sitting fee**, and a complimentary 5x7.
- **A choice of two session styles**, picked when they book:
  - *Business and Branding.* An efficient sitting that produces a professional headshot on

  - *Family and Legacy.* A longer, guided sitting for members bringing spouses, children,

- **A directed sitting.** Julian guides every pose, so members don't need to know how to
  pose.

### How long does each sitting take?

Between 20 and 120 minutes, depending on the session style and how many people are in the
frame. Business and Branding sittings are the shortest. Family and Legacy sittings run
longest because the review happens in the same visit.

### Do members have to buy anything?

No. There is no sitting fee, and the club-edit retouch of each directory portrait is
complimentary. Prints and digital files are optional, with packages from $150 and no
minimums.

### Where do the portraits go?

One directory portrait per member goes to the club for its membership album, point-of-sale
system, and staff training directory. The rest of the sitting belongs to the member.

### How do members book?

Through a booking page made for your club, or by form, email, text, or phone. Each member
receives a confirmation email and a courtesy text offering a consultation before the
sitting.

### Can families be photographed together?

Yes. Members choose the Family and Legacy style when they book, and spouses, children, and
grandchildren are all welcome in the frame.

### Are the portraits retouched?

Yes. Every directory portrait gets a complimentary club-edit that softens unwanted details
but keeps the member recognizable to staff. Members can buy further retouching for personal
use.
