# About Arceo Studio Collective

Arceo Studio Collective runs on-site member portrait programs for private clubs. The studio
was founded by Julian Arceo in FILL and is based in FILL.

<!-- H2 -->
## What we do

We set up in a private room at a club and photograph its members against one consistent
hand-painted backdrop. The club receives a directory portrait of each member for its
membership roster, point-of-sale system, and staff training directory, so staff can greet
members by name from day one, and members can put faces to the names they see.

Members pay no sitting fee. They choose a Business and Branding or a Family and Legacy
session when they book, and they review their portraits with Julian in the same visit.
Prints and digital files are optional, with packages from $150 and no minimums.

[How a program works at your club →](/private-clubs)

<!-- H2 -->
## How we work

- **Recognizable over perfect.** Every directory portrait gets a complimentary club-edit:
  enough retouching to look good, and never so much that staff can't recognize the member
  at the door.
- **Directed, not posed.** Members don't need to know how to pose. Julian directs every
  sitting.
- **Consistent.** One backdrop and one setup across the whole membership, so the directory
  reads as one collection rather than a pile of snapshots.
