## At a sitting

Almost everyone says they photograph badly. Julian's answer is that posing isn't the
member's job. He directs every sitting himself, guiding each member through a range of
curated poses, then sits down with them in the same visit to review the results.

Every directory portrait gets a club-edit, retouched enough to look its best while staying
recognizable to the staff who will greet that member at the door.
