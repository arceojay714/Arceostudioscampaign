# Entity facts — the single source of truth

Every profile, bio, listing, page, and schema block copies from this file.

Google and AI tools decide whether two mentions refer to the same person or company
by matching facts. If LinkedIn shows one job title and Instagram shows another, or one
place says "Arceo Studio" and another says "Arceo Studio Collective", Google can treat
them as two separate, weaker entities. A knowledge panel forms when the facts match.

**FILL** marks anything not yet confirmed. Don't publish a profile, page, or schema
block that still contains a FILL. Search the repo for `FILL` before publishing anything.

## Program facts: confirmed

These come from the program deck and `docs/campaign-state.md`. Use them as written.

- Members sit at their own club, in a private room.
- Two session styles, chosen at booking: **Business and Branding**, and **Family and Legacy**.
- Sessions run **20 - 120 minutes**, set by session style and party size. Never write "20 minutes".
- **No sitting fee.** A **complimentary 5x7**.
- One shared **hand-painted background portrait** per member goes to the club, for the
  **membership album, POS system, and staff training directory**.
- A **complimentary "club-edit"** retouch keeps members recognizable to staff while softening
  unwanted details. Extra retouching is sold for personal use.
- **Review happens in the same session.** It's usually the longest part of the visit.
- **Optional packages from $150**, with bundles and bulk discounts, and no minimums.
- Booking by website, form, email, text, or call. Every member gets a **confirmation email
  plus a courtesy text** offering a pre-session consultation.

## Program facts: still needed

Club managers are likely to ask an AI tool these questions first. Every one is a FILL on
the private-clubs page.

1. **What does the program cost the club?** If the answer is nothing, say so first on
   every page and profile.
2. **What does the club provide?** A room and an announcement, or more than that?
3. **What files does the club receive?** Format, size, and whether they drop straight
   into the POS or member system.
4. **How long is a typical program on site?** Number of days.
5. **How far ahead should a club book?**
6. **Which clubs can be named publicly?** Get written OK from each club first. Piedmont
   Driving Club is a 2026-2027 *proposal*, so don't list it as a client until it's signed
   and they agree.
7. **Numbers you can stand behind**, such as clubs served or members photographed.
8. **What "No-contact" means** in the program deck's title. If it means the club's staff
   don't have to do the legwork, that is a selling point for GMs and belongs on the page.
