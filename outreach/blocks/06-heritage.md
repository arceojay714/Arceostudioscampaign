# Block: heritage and milestone years

Only for clubs with a verified anniversary or a genuine claim to age. **Verify the founding
year against the club's own materials before it appears in a letter** — getting a club's
own history wrong in the opening sentence is a self-inflicted disqualification.

> A club marking its [Nth] year has spent a century collecting minutes, ledgers, and
> trophies, and almost no photographs of the people. The anniversary is the natural moment
> to fix that, and the portraits made this year are the ones the club will still be hanging
> in fifty.

Pairs naturally with a commemorative deliverable: a bound directory, a wall, a display for
the anniversary event. That's a larger engagement than a portrait week and worth naming as
a possibility, not a price.
