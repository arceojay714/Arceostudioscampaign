# Block: the directory argument

> Most club directories list a name, a join year, and a phone number. Members can look up
> anyone and still not recognize them across the dining room. A portrait beside the name
> is the difference between a roster and a membership that knows itself.

Variants:

- **Roster-to-membership:** "The directory already knows who your members are. It just
  can't show them."
- **New-member framing:** "A new member spends their first year quietly guessing at names.
  A directory with faces in it shortens that to a week."
- **Institutional-memory framing:** "In thirty years the club will have every ledger and
  minute from this era, and no photographs of the people who were here."

Do not stack these. Pick one.
