# Block: how the week runs

For warm replies, when they've started picturing the day.

> We set up in one room for the week. Members book their own slot online, so nothing lands
> on your staff. Sittings run about fifteen minutes. We bring everything — lighting,
> backdrop, and the booking page — and leave the room as we found it. Retouching is
> included, and proofs go out inside a week.

Adjust the duration and the week length to the club's size. For a club under 300 members,
say three days rather than a week; overstating scale makes a small club feel like a bad fit.
