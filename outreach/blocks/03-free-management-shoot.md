# Block: the free management shoot — Tier A only

The strongest opener we have, and it only works where Jay can drive. Use it in every Tier A
approach, in person where possible.

> Before any of this, I'd like to photograph your management team, at the club, at no
> charge and with no obligation. You'll have finished portraits of your GM, your department
> heads, and your board officers to use however you like. If the work isn't what you want
> for your members, you've lost a morning and gained a set of staff portraits.

Why it beats a proposal:

- **It removes the risk entirely.** The club is not deciding about a member program. It's
  deciding whether to accept something free.
- **It gets Julian in the building.** Every club that has met him is a materially different
  prospect from one that has read about him.
- **The decision-makers become the subjects.** A GM who has been well photographed and
  directed by someone who made it painless has experienced the product, not read a claim
  about it. `posing-is-not-your-job` stops being a headline and becomes something that
  happened to them.
- **It produces a local reference.** Staff portraits go on the website and in the
  newsletter, and the next club sees them.

Delivery notes: bring printed proofs back in person rather than emailing a gallery. The
print quality is the argument for the print business, and the second visit is the meeting
where the member program actually gets discussed.

Cost to us is a morning and some prints. Treat it as marketing spend, not as free work,
and do not offer it outside Tier A — a free shoot that requires a flight is a loss.
