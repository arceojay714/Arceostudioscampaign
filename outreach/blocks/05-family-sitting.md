# Block: the family sitting

Turns a directory obligation into something a member actually wants, and it is where the
order value is.

> A member's sitting doesn't have to be a headshot. Spouse, children, grandchildren —
> every one of them is welcome in the frame, at the same appointment. For a lot of
> families it's the first proper portrait they've had made together.

Strongest at clubs with junior programs, family memberships, pools, or summer camps. This
is also the block that separates us from the headshot vendors, who do not offer it at all.
