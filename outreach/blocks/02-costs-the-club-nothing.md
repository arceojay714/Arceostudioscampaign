# Block: what it costs the club

The block that gets a board to yes. The club is not approving an expense.

> There is no cost to the club and no line item to approve. Members sit at no charge and
> keep a complimentary 5x7. The club provides a room for the week and a mention in the
> member newsletter. Anything a member wants beyond the complimentary print, they buy
> directly from us.

Why this works: a GM can say yes without a board vote, a budget cycle, or a purchasing
process. It converts a six-month institutional decision into a scheduling decision.

Keep the three obligations explicit — room, newsletter mention, nothing else. Vagueness
here reads as a hidden ask and stalls the yes.
