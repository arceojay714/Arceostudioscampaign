# Block: terms, insurance, and the boring things that close deals

Second touch onward, or any club with a real purchasing process. Clubs are institutional
buyers; the first thing a GM asks after "yes" is a certificate of insurance.

> We carry general liability and can name the club as additional insured on the
> certificate. Member images are used only for the club's directory and only with the
> member's written consent. We're happy to work under the club's own vendor agreement.

Have staged and ready before this block is ever sent:

- Certificate of insurance, with the club addable as additional insured
- W-9
- A short services agreement covering scope, dates, cancellation, and image rights
- A member consent/model release, with a clear opt-out for directory-only use
- Any club-specific vendor form, once we know the club's process

Being able to answer all five inside a day is a genuine competitive advantage over a solo
photographer who cannot, and it costs nothing to prepare in advance.
