## Letter — print version (preferred)

Dear [NAME],

A hundred years is a rare thing for a club to be able to say, and I imagine you have been
planning for it for a while.

I run a portrait studio here in Olympia, and I work with clubs on the part of an anniversary
that usually gets left until last. A club that has been here since 1926 has kept every
ledger, every minute book, and every trophy from those hundred years, and almost no
photographs of the people. The directory lists names and join years. Members can look up
anyone and still not recognize them across the dining room.

There is no cost to the club and no line item to approve. Members sit at no charge and keep
a complimentary 5x7. The club provides a room for the week and a mention in the member
newsletter, and nothing else. Anything a member wants beyond the complimentary print, they
buy from us directly.

Before any of that, I would like to photograph your management team, at the club, at no
charge and with no obligation. You would have finished portraits of yourself, your
department heads, and your board officers to use however you like — the website, the
newsletter, the anniversary materials. If the work is not what you want for your members,
you have lost a morning and gained a set of staff portraits.

I am ten minutes away. Could I photograph your team one morning in the next few weeks?

Julian Arceo
Arceo Studio Collective
[postal address]
[phone] · [email]
