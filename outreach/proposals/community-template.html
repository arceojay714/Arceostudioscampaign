What this is

The program

A directory program that runs start to finish without cost or work for the church.

Most congregations are overdue, and it is not their fault

The company that photographed church directories for most of the country closed its church division in 2021. A great many congregations have not been able to refresh a directory since, even though the usual cycle is two to three years.

  • No cost to the church

    The studio covers its own setup, staffing, editing, and production. There is no fee to run the program.

  • No cost to members

    Sitting for a portrait is free, and so is the finished portrait each participant takes home.

  • A complimentary portrait for everyone who participates

    It is the church's gift to the member, and it tends to be the main reason people take part.

How the program is funded

What members pay for, and what they do not

Since the program costs the church and its members nothing, here is how it works. Members who would like additional prints or pieces for their homes can purchase them, with options starting around $150, and there is never any pressure or obligation. Most members buy more because they enjoy the experience, not because they were sold. Every participant keeps a complimentary portrait at no charge either way.

Almost no lift on your side

The studio handles scheduling, setup, the sessions, editing, and delivery. Reaching out to the congregation stays entirely in the church's hands, and a short note from the office alongside the complimentary portrait is usually enough to drive strong participation.

The residency

How it works

A simple path, start to finish.

We set aside a four to six week period at the church and work through the congregation during that time. There is no fixed date to commit to, so the window is built around what works for [CHURCH NAME]. Members opt in and book on their own time, and the church sets the pace.

  1. The church announces

    A short rollout through the church's own approved channels.

  2. Members opt in

    They book on their own time. No cold calls needed.

  3. The session

    Guided and unhurried, held on the church campus. Families welcome.

  4. The gift

    A complimentary portrait and a private online gallery the member keeps.

  5. Delivered

    Directory images, on a one-week turnaround.

What we ask of the church

A short announcement

A note from the office letting members know the program is coming and that it helps the church.

A comfortable on-site room

A quiet space on campus for the sessions during the window.

One staff contact

A single point of contact to coordinate logistics with the studio.

No roster required

Members reach out and book on their own, so the church never has to hand over a membership list. Everything else, from setup through delivery, sits with the studio.

What changes for the other two segments

Same seven pages. Only the lines below swap.

Senior living
  • Addressed to the Life Enrichment or Activity Director, not an administrator.
  • "Resident" throughout, not member.
  • Drop the 2021 vendor callout. Replace with the resident-and-family framing: the portrait goes home to children and grandchildren.
  • Add a consent paragraph on page 5. Consent comes from the resident, or the responsible party where the resident cannot give it, never from community staff.
  • Intake option A becomes a family email, since adult children are usually the ones who act on it.
  • Add background check and vaccination compliance to "what you can count on".
55+ and HOA communities
  • Addressed to the activities chair or lifestyle director.
  • "Homeowner" or "resident" throughout.
  • "No roster required" carries less weight here, since HOA rosters are semi-public. Lead instead on no lift for staff or volunteers.
  • Directory becomes the community directory or resident guide.
  • Clubhouse is the venue. Intake runs through the community newsletter and the clubhouse board.
Before this goes out
  • Swap the three plates for the senior portraits. The club images do not fit this audience.
  • Decide whether the club list stays. It reads as credibility to a church, but a church or community reference would land harder. Worth adding one as soon as there is one.
  • Confirm the $150 entry point holds for this segment.
