Before You Come

Questions

What should I wear?
Solid colors photograph best, and darker tones read well against the directory layout. Coat and tie are required for gentlemen aged 14 and older in The Main Clubhouse, so most members sit in what they arrive in.
How long does it take?
Twenty minutes is the scheduled slot. Most members are finished sooner.
May I bring my family?
Yes. Spouse, children, and grandchildren are all welcome. Book one slot and note how many are attending.
I photograph badly. Is this going to work?
It is the most common thing members say, and it is the part you are not responsible for. Julian directs the sitting — posture, angle, and expression are his job.
Who sees the portrait?
You do, first and alone. You select the frame that goes on file. Nothing enters the directory without your approval.
Is there a charge?
There is no sitting fee, and one complimentary 5x7 is included. Additional prints and files may be purchased at the sitting, but nothing further is required of you.
What if I cannot make any of the times?
Call the Front Desk and a time will be arranged. Sittings are being scheduled around member availability wherever the calendar allows.
