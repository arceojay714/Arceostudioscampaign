Before You Come

Questions

How long does it take?
Between 20 and 120 minutes, depending on the session style you choose and how many people are in the frame. Business and Branding sittings are the quickest. Family and Legacy sittings run longest, because the review happens in the same visit.
Which session style should I choose?
Business and Branding if you want an efficient sitting and a headshot you can use professionally. Family and Legacy if you are bringing people with you, or want the longer guided experience with a full review.
What should I wear?
Coat and tie are required for gentlemen aged 14 and older in The Main Clubhouse, and The Georgian Room sits within it. Every booking also comes with a personal text from your photographer offering advice ahead of the day, so you can ask then.
May I bring my family?
Yes. Spouse, children, and grandchildren are every one of them welcome in the frame. Choose the Family and Legacy style when you book and note how many are attending.
I photograph badly. Is this going to work?
It is the most common thing members say, and it is the part you are not responsible for. Julian guides and directs you through a range of curated poses — posture, angle, and expression are his job, not yours.
Is there a charge?
There is no sitting fee, and the club-edit retouch of your directory portrait is complimentary. Prints and digital files are entirely optional, with tailored packages beginning at $150 and no minimums.
Where does my portrait go?
One shared background portrait goes to the Club for the membership album, the POS system, and the staff training directory. Everything else from your sitting is yours.
